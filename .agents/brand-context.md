# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: The Community Pack
- **Category**: Grassroots community nonprofit / mutual aid — child & family basic-needs relief
- **Description**: Neighbors providing backpacks, school supplies, clothing, and everyday essentials to children and families across Beaufort, Jasper, and Hampton counties, SC — 365 days a year.
- **Stage**: Early. Operating and taking requests, but volunteer-run out of a home porch and personal garages. Not a registered 501(c)(3), so donations are not tax-deductible.
- **Website**: https://communitypack.org/

## Audience
The brand serves two distinct audiences and must speak to both without letting either overhear the wrong tone.

- **Primary Audience**:
  1. **Families who need help** — parents and caregivers of school-age children in Beaufort, Jasper, and especially Hampton County living at or below the poverty line. Often without the time, gas money, or transportation for an hours-long drive to a distant giveaway. Many are quietly struggling and reluctant to ask.
  2. **Neighbors who can give** — local residents, church groups, small businesses, and school staff across the Lowcountry who want to help visibly and locally, and want to know their donation reached an actual child nearby.
- **Key Problem**:
  - *For families*: The gap between what a child needs for school and what the household can cover. The average family spends $864 to send one child back to school; for many local families that money simply isn't there. Existing aid means long lines, long drives, and no guarantee supplies remain when you reach the front.
  - *For givers*: Wanting to help but not knowing where donations actually land, or whether they stayed in the community.
- **Their Language**: "Neighbors helping neighbors." "No judgment. No hoops. Just help." "New or gently used." "Sizes, colors, supplies." "You're not alone in this." "The exact size, the exact shoe." "Drop-off." "Drive." "Tell us what your child needs." "We keep every request private."

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Proximity and dignity. No line, no long drive, no eligibility gauntlet — a family fills out a short form on their phone and the request gets matched to a donation, one at a time, privately. Local end to end: collected in Bluffton, carried up to Hampton County by the same volunteers. Year-round rather than a once-a-year back-to-school push. The founder's own porch is the warehouse, which makes the operation legibly personal rather than institutional.
- **Competitors**: Not competitors so much as the alternatives a family weighs. (1) Large regional/metro giveaway events — e.g. the Savannah supply-and-uniform giveaway that prompted the brand's founding: high volume, first-come-first-served, supplies run out. (2) National-brand charities and their local chapters — Salvation Army, United Way partners, Toys for Tots-style seasonal drives: credible and tax-deductible, but seasonal, form-heavy, and not Hampton-County-specific. (3) School-based and church-based assistance — closest in spirit and the most likely partner rather than rival.
- **Market Position**: Hyper-local niche. Deliberately small, personal, and fast-moving where the large organizations are broad, seasonal, and procedural.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Warm, plainspoken, dignified, dependable, neighborly
- **Tone**: Casual and human, never institutional or clinical. Serious about the need, gentle in the telling. Short declarative sentences. Speaks *to* a person, not *about* a population. Two register shifts to respect: with families, reassurance and zero judgment ("You don't have to wait in a line"); with donors, specificity and honesty about the gap ("That's the gap we fill"). Statistics are used sparingly and always resolved back to a neighbor's kid.
- **Voice Admires**: Not stated by the founder. The site's own execution points toward the plainspoken, hand-lettered, porch-front register of local mutual aid and small-town church bulletins rather than any national charity's voice — handwritten Patrick Hand display type over a warm cream ground (#fbf3e6), deep Lowcountry blue (#2c5b7e), clay orange (#d9502e), and gold (#f2a730).

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**:
  - **Dignity** — no child should have to notice their family is struggling; no parent should have to perform need to receive help.
  - **Proximity** — help should come from down the road, not two counties over.
  - **Privacy** — every request is kept private, always.
  - **Constancy** — 365 days a year, not one week in August.
  - **Honesty** — plain about what the org is and isn't, including its 501(c)(3) status.
- **Mission**: The Community Pack supports children and families 365 days a year by providing resources, encouragement, and a helping hand when it's needed most — so children can keep learning, growing, and moving forward even when life gets hard, and so every family knows they are not alone.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Move from a porch-and-garage operation to a sustainable, year-round one serving all three counties. The three stated near-term needs are: (1) secure a dedicated storage space, replacing volunteers' garages; (2) extend regular delivery routes deeper into Hampton County; (3) partner with more local schools so needs are identified directly rather than waiting for families to find the form.
- **Key Metrics**: Families and children served; support requests received and fulfilled (and time-to-fulfillment); donation volume by category (backpacks, supplies, clothing, hygiene, birthday, monetary); active volunteers and repeat volunteers; school and business/church partnerships secured; Facebook reach and page shares, which are currently the primary discovery channel for both families and donors.
